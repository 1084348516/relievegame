About Tap Tap Tap

Tap Tap Tap is a free tile matching puzzle game you can play right in your browser, on desktop or mobile, with no download and no sign-up. Cute animal and fruit tiles are stacked in layers on the board. Tap the tiles you can reach, collect three of the same kind, and watch them disappear. It is a quick, relaxing way to take a break, relieve stress and give your brain a little workout.

How to Play

  1. Tap any tile that is not covered by another tile. Covered tiles are greyed out until the tiles on top of them are cleared.
  2. The tile moves into the slot bar below the board. When three identical tiles land in the bar, they match and vanish.
  3. The bar holds seven tiles. If it fills up before you can make a match, the game is over and you start again from level 1.
  4. Clear every tile on the board to pass the level. Beat all three levels to enter your name in the Hall of Fame.

Tools That Help

  • Hold moves the last tile from the bar into a spare spot, freeing up space. Press it again to bring the tile back.
  • Undo takes back your last move, so one careless tap does not cost you the whole round.
  • Restart starts a fresh game from level 1 whenever you want a clean board.

Tips for Clearing the Board

Look before you tap. Clear the top layers first so more tiles become available, and try to keep only two or three different tile types in the bar at a time. Save Hold and Undo for the moments when the bar is nearly full. Later levels use more tile types and deeper stacks, so planning a few moves ahead makes a big difference.
